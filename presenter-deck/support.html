Frequently asked questions

How do I activate the screen canvas?
Press ⌥ Option + D to toggle the full-screen drawing layer, then pick a pen, laser pointer, arrow, or shape from the toolbar. Use ⌘ + Z to undo and ⎋ Esc to exit.
How do I use the mouse spotlight?
Press ⌥ Option + S to dim the background around the cursor. Press ⌥ Option + S, ⎋ Esc, or right-click to exit.
How does the keystroke HUD work?
Press ⌥ Option + K to show a floating shortcut HUD. Key combos such as ⌘ + C appear at the bottom of the screen during tutorials and recordings.
How do I use the presentation timer?
Press ⌥ Option + T to toggle a floating timer that stays visible without blocking your slides.
Why are Accessibility and Input Monitoring permissions needed?
Accessibility enables global hotkeys while presenting over other apps. Input Monitoring powers the optional keystroke HUD. Everything stays local: PresenterDeck is 100% offline and never stores or transmits keystrokes.

常见问题

如何开启屏幕画布？
按 ⌥ Option + D 切换全屏绘制图层，然后从工具栏选择画笔、激光笔、箭头或图形。按 ⌘ + Z 撤销，按 ⎋ Esc 退出。
如何使用鼠标聚光灯？
按 ⌥ Option + S 暗化光标周围的背景。按 ⌥ Option + S、⎋ Esc 或右键即可退出。
按键提示浮层如何使用？
按 ⌥ Option + K 显示浮动快捷键提示。演示或录屏时，⌘ + C 等组合键会显示在屏幕底部。
演示计时器如何使用？
按 ⌥ Option + T 切换浮动计时器，计时不会遮挡幻灯片内容。
为什么需要辅助功能与输入监控权限？
辅助功能用于在其他应用之上演示时启用全局快捷键；输入监控用于可选的按键提示浮层。所有处理都在本机完成：PresenterDeck 100% 离线，绝不存储或上传按键内容。
