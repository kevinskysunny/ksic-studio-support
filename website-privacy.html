Website privacy

Updated September 5, 2026. This notice covers this support website; each app has its own linked privacy policy.

Browsing and preferences

This site adds no advertising pixels, analytics SDKs, account registration or contact forms. It stores your chosen language in browser local storage. You can clear it through your browser’s site-data settings. Existing documentation may have separate language preferences.

Hosting and external links

GitHub Pages hosts these pages and may process IP addresses and request metadata to deliver and secure them. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Following an App Store link takes you to Apple, whose policies govern purchases and downloads. We do not receive your payment-card information through this site.

Support email

If you email support, the recipient and email service process your address, message and any attachments to handle your request. Do not send passwords, payment information or private customer data. Send questions about your support correspondence or a deletion request to the address you contacted; applicable retention requirements may apply.

Website questions: kssicstudio@gmail.com.

网站隐私说明

更新于 2026 年 9 月 5 日。本说明适用于此支持网站，各应用的数据处理方式请查看其独立隐私政策。

浏览与偏好

本网站没有新增广告像素、统计 SDK、注册或联系表单。所选语言保存在浏览器本地存储中，可通过浏览器的网站数据设置清除。既有文档可能使用独立语言偏好。

托管与外部链接

本网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，托管方可能处理 IP 地址和请求元数据以提供页面及保障安全，详见 GitHub 隐私声明。点击 App Store 链接后将进入 Apple 服务，购买与下载适用其政策。本网站不会接收你的支付卡信息。

支持邮件

发送支持邮件时，收件人和邮件服务会处理你的邮箱地址、正文和附件，以处理请求。请勿发送密码、支付信息或客户隐私数据。如需查询或请求删除支持邮件，请联系原收件地址；适用的数据保留要求仍可能生效。

网站问题请联系 kssicstudio@gmail.com。
