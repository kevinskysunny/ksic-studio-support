KSIC Studio / macOS 14.0+

Keep your work links together.

Organize, search and open your websites and work resources from a local-first Mac workspace.

On a Mac, use “Open App Store on your Mac” to request the app in the App Store. Availability and purchase depend on your Apple Account region. On other devices, view the app here and install later on your Mac.

KSIC Studio · v1.0.0 · Store information verified September 5, 2026

Open support guide Open App Store on your Mac

What it does

  • Group and search your work links
  • Open recent and featured links from the menu bar
  • Import configuration and back up your workspace

Before you start

Opens links in your default browser. HTTPS reachability checks run when requested. This is a link launcher, not a cloud sync or team collaboration service.

Use the Mac App Store listing for purchase, downloads, updates and current pricing.
