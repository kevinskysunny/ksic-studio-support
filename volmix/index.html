VolMix / macOS 14.2+

Control one app’s sound.

Adjust volume and mute for detected audio apps from your Mac menu bar.

On a Mac, use “Open App Store on your Mac” to request the app in the App Store. Availability and purchase depend on your Apple Account region. On other devices, view the app here and install later on your Mac.

Open support guide Open App Store on your Mac

What it does

  • Independent volume from 0% to 100%
  • App mute and live audio indicators
  • System output selection in the same panel

Before you start

Requires system-audio capture permission. Protected audio and some routing or devices may not work. Controls apps, not browser tabs; no amplification above 100% or per-app output routing.

Use the Mac App Store listing for purchase, downloads, updates and current pricing.

VolMix / macOS 14.2+

单独调整一个应用的声音。

在 Mac 菜单栏中，为检测到的音频应用独立调音或静音。

在 Mac 上请点击“在 Mac 上打开 App Store”前往应用详情。能否下载与购买取决于 Apple 账户地区及当地上架状态。其他设备可先查看本页，再到 Mac 上安装。

打开使用帮助 在 Mac 上打开 App Store

可以做什么

  • 0%–100% 独立音量调节
  • 应用静音与实时发声指示
  • 在同一面板选择系统输出设备

使用前了解

需要系统音频捕获权限。受保护音频、部分路由或设备可能不兼容。按应用而非浏览器标签页控制；不支持超过 100% 放大或按应用分配输出设备。

购买、下载、更新及当前价格请以 Mac App Store 页面为准。
