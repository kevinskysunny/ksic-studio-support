Frequently asked questions

Why can’t I see an app in the VolMix list?
Only apps that are currently running appear in the list. If an app plays audio through a protected or system-level audio path, it may not be adjustable. Try relaunching the app after granting VolMix the audio permission it requests on first launch.

Why is there no sound when I lower an app below 100%?
Some apps route audio through a process or device that VolMix cannot intercept reliably. Lowering the volume silences their audio path in those cases. A relaunch of the target app or switching its output device usually restores a working mix. If it happens with a specific app, tell us the app name and macOS version so we can reproduce it.

Does VolMix record or upload my audio?
No. Audio is captured in memory with Apple’s native CoreAudio process-tap technology, mixed locally through a private aggregate device, and never recorded, saved, uploaded, or shared.

What apps are supported?
Browsers, video players, games, and communication apps that use standard system audio output. Apps with protected audio paths may not be adjustable.

How do I switch the app language?
Open VolMix from the menu bar, go to Settings, and choose English, Simplified Chinese, or Follow System. The change applies immediately.

How do I restore my purchase?
Open the purchase screen in VolMix and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account used for the purchase.

常见问题

为什么列表里看不到某个 App？
只有正在运行的 App 会出现在列表里。如果某款应用通过受保护或系统级音频通道播放声音，可能无法调节。首次启动时请允许 VolMix 的音频权限；如仍不显示，可尝试重新启动该应用。

为什么把音量调到 100% 以下就没有声音了？
部分应用通过 VolMix 无法可靠拦截的进程或设备输出音频，此时调低音量会直接静音其音频通道。重新启动目标应用或切换其输出设备，通常可恢复正常的混音效果。如果特定应用总是这样，请把应用名称和 macOS 版本发给我们以便复现。

VolMix 会录制或上传我的音频吗？
不会。音频通过 Apple 原生 CoreAudio Process Tap 技术在内存中捕获，在本地通过私有聚合设备混音，绝不会被录制、保存、上传或分享。

支持哪些应用？
浏览器、视频播放器、游戏以及使用标准系统音频输出的通讯应用。使用受保护音频通道的应用可能无法调节。

如何切换 App 语言？
点击菜单栏的 VolMix 图标，进入设置，选择“英文”“简体中文”或“跟随系统”，切换即时生效。

如何恢复购买？
请使用购买时的同一 Apple 账户登录，在 VolMix 的购买页面选择“恢复购买”。
